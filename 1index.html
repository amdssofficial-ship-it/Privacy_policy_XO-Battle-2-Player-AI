User Data Deletion Information

We respect your privacy. This page explains what happens to information when you play XO Battle and how to request deletion.

XO Battle does not ask you to create an account or provide your name, email address, phone number, or other personal details. The game build does not send player profile information or gameplay scores to a server operated by the game developer.

Information handled by the game

XO Battle uses the Facebook Instant Games platform to start and run the game. The game code does not request your Facebook profile name, profile photo, friends list, or Facebook user ID. Game scores are used during the current play session and are not uploaded to a developer-operated server.

Facebook/Meta may process information under its own terms and privacy policies when you use its platform. For details about information Meta processes and how to manage it, visit Meta's Privacy Policy.

How to request deletion

Because XO Battle does not maintain a developer-side account or personal-data database for players, there is no player profile or gameplay record held by the game developer for us to delete.

  1. Close XO Battle.
  2. If you want to clear locally stored browser data, use your device or browser settings to clear the site data for the game.
  3. To manage or request deletion of information associated with your Facebook account, use Facebook Help Centre and Meta's account privacy controls.

Questions

For questions about data handled by Facebook or the Facebook Instant Games platform, please contact Meta through the Facebook Help Centre.
