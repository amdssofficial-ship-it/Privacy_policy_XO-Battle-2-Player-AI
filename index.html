1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile game XO Battle – 2 Player & AI ("the Application"), developed and published as a free, ad-supported game. This page informs players and users of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information when using our Application.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. By downloading, accessing, or playing XO Battle – 2 Player & AI, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information Collection and Usage

XO Battle – 2 Player & AI does not require account creation, registration, or login. We do not directly collect, transmit, store, or sell any personally identifiable information (such as your legal name, physical address, email address, contact list, or phone number).

Local Data Only: All gameplay progress, including match statistics, win/loss/draw scores, round counters, board size selections, and audio/vibration preferences, is stored strictly on your local device. This data never leaves your device and is not synchronized to any developer-owned remote server.

3. Device Permissions & Purpose

The Application requests only the minimal set of Android system permissions required for core functionality and advertisement delivery:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required to establish a network connection to load and display advertisements provided by Google AdMob.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Allows the advertising SDK to check whether an active internet connection is available before requesting ads, preventing unnecessary network errors and battery drain.
  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Used solely to generate tactile haptic vibrations for move placements, UI clicks, and celebratory win animations. This permission accesses only the device vibrator motor and has no access to private data.

Notice: The Application does not access your camera, microphone, precise GPS location, contacts, device storage, or file system.
